# Archive: every skill we evaluated

Nothing here is deleted. A skill moves here when it leaves the Top 20, and every new candidate gets an entry here too, in or out. So anyone browsing sees the full picture: what we looked at, what made it, what did not, and why.

The only things that skip the archive are trash: dead repos with no README, broken installs that cannot be fixed, spam, or duplicates with nothing new. Those are not worth the bytes.
